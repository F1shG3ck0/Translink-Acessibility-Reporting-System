What percentage of stops have accessibility features?

Below is a chart showing the distribution of accessible and non-accessible stops across all of Translink's stops. This report aims to show what stops need to be improved in terms of accessibility.


Percentage of Accessible Stops


This visual report highlights the overall proportion of stops across the network that currently provide at least one accessibility feature. By presenting the data as a doughnut chart, it becomes immediately clear whether accessibility is widespread across the network or not. A low percentage would indicate that many passengers (particularly those with mobility impairments, visual impairments, or additional support needs) may face barriers when navigating the network. This insight can guide investment decisions by identifying the scale of improvement required and helping planners prioritise upgrades that would deliver the greatest accessibility impact.
