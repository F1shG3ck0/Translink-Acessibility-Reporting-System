Routes with Hospital Stops



This report shows that a limited number of routes which currently provide direct access to hospital facilities, with many services relying on a small number of key stops such as City Hospital and Royal Victoria Hospital. This concentration may create pressure on specific routes and interchange points, particularly for users with restricted mobility or time‑critical healthcare needs. The report can therefore be used to identify opportunities where additional hospital‑serving stops or route adjustments could improve network resilience and accessibility.
